# ViganGo: Local Activities and Tourism Discovery Platform for Vigan and Metro Vigan


The Domain: Tourism Technology / Web and Mobile Information Systems

## Overview

A web/mobile application that helps tourists and residents discover activities and experiences available in Vigan and Metro Vigan. Users can browse heritage sites, cultural activities, festivals, food experiences, recreational activities, local products, and nearby destinations. Each listing provides relevant information such as location, operating hours, schedule, estimated duration, entrance fees, and a description. Users can filter activities according to their interests, available time, and budget.

## Problem

Information about activities and destinations in Vigan and Metro Vigan is distributed across tourism websites, social-media pages, business pages, and word of mouth. Visitors may know popular destinations such as Calle Crisologo but may not know what activities are available, when they are available, how much they cost, or what other experiences they can combine during their visit.
